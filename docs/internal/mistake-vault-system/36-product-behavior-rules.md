## 1. Candidate Trust & Behavioral Guarantees

The Mistake Vault guarantees consistent, predictable behavior across all candidate interactions:

### Rule 1: No Silent Deletion
Mistakes are never deleted or hidden due to inactivity. A candidate returning after 6 months will find their full mistake history intact, accurately scheduled with refreshed decay recommendations.

### Rule 2: Inviolable Two-Consecutive Mastery
No candidate can bypass the 2-consecutive correct requirement. Single-correct answers always result in the intermediate `REVISITING` state.

### Rule 3: Explainable Urgency
Every item surfaced in the "Due for Revision" or "Next Best Revision" queue displays an explicit, human-readable rationale (e.g., *"Repeated slip (3x) needs immediate practice"* or *"Refresh recommended — mastered 18 days ago"*).

### Rule 4: Zero Penalty for Relapse
When a mastered question is failed in a future mock test, the candidate's historical streak count is reset, but past mastery achievements remain recognized in lifetime longitudinal analytics.

### Rule 5: Immediate Gamified Feedback
Successfully completing a remediation drill awards 5 Courage Coins per resolved mistake, reinforcing active study habits.
