## 1. The Pedagogical Crisis in Competitive Examination Preparation

In high-stakes competitive examinations (such as UPSC, SSC CGL, Banking, and State Public Service Commissions), candidates solve thousands of practice questions. Traditional preparation workflows suffer from three systemic flaws:

1. **The Passive Error Illusion**: Candidates review an answer explanation once, feel an immediate sensation of understanding, but experience total memory decay when re-tested under exam pressure 14 days later.
2. **The "Bucket of Shame" Syndrome**: Traditional error notebooks accumulate hundreds of unstructured questions, overwhelming the candidate with an unprioritized backlog that causes anxiety and avoidance.
3. **Cognitive Blindness**: Candidates treat all wrong answers identically, failing to distinguish between theoretical ignorance (`CONCEPTUAL_GAP`), operational haste (`TIME_PANIC`), and wording traps (`DISTRACTOR_TRAP`).
