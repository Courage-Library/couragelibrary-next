Changes

Login logic, Google sign-in, Cloudflare protection and the eligibility rule (now worded via the Terms) all stay — only layout, wording and when things appear change.

  1. 1
    Brand panel (desktop only)Blue band with the tagline and 3 true features. Hidden on phones so the form comes first.
  2. 2
    Quiet notices"You've been signed out" as a small grey notice that fades after a few seconds, instead of a big teal box.
  3. 3
    Honest heading"Sign in to continue your preparation" — no promise of notes or study plans we don't have. Charcoal heading, Space Blue button (our palette).
  4. 4
    Clean order, no age box on sign-inGoogle → "or sign in with email" → email + password → Sign in. Existing users are never asked anything extra.
  5. 5
    Password helpersShow/hide eye, "Forgot password?" next to the label, no red asterisks, email keyboard on phones, autofill works.
  6. 6
    Cloudflare in invisible modeA small "Protected by Cloudflare" line. The widget appears only when Cloudflare needs a check.
  7. 7
    Clear errorsMessage under the field in words (coral + icon, not colour only). Never says which of email/password was wrong.
  8. 8
    Friendly security checkIf verification fails: plain explanation, Retry, and a hint about VPN/ad-blockers — instead of "Verification failed".
  9. 9
    One friendly consent line on sign-up"I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy, and confirm I'm eligible to create an account" — no age number on screen; the eligibility rule lives in the Terms. Covers both Google and email.
  10. 10
    Password strength hint"At least 8 characters" + a simple strength bar. Same design for Forgot password and Reset password pages.
