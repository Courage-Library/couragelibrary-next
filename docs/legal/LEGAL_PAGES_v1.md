## 3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This policy applies to paid plans bought on couragelibrary.in. Free features need no payment and have nothing to refund.

### 3.1 Cancelling a plan

- Plans are one-time purchases for a fixed period (for example 3, 6 or 12 months). They do not renew automatically, so you are never charged again unless you choose to buy again.
- Access ends on the date shown in Account → Billing. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before.
- When a plan ends, your data (attempts, Mistake Vault, Memory Lock, notes) is kept; only Pro features stop until you renew.

### 3.2 When you get a full refund

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| You were charged twice for the same plan | Full refund of the extra charge |
| You paid but Pro was not activated within 24 hours, and we cannot fix it | Full refund |
| Money left your account but the payment shows as failed | Usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days; if not, we refund it in full |
| A paid feature you bought is removed during your plan | Pro-rata refund for the unused period |
| You ask within 7 days of your first purchase of a plan and have attempted no more than 1 full mock test on Pro | Full refund (once per user) |

### 3.3 When a refund is not given

- After the 7-day window above, or once the usage limit in it is crossed, payments for the current period are not refundable.
- No refund for a period already used, or for an account closed because it broke the Terms of Service (sharing, scraping, misuse).
- Coins and rewards have no cash value and are not refunded.

### 3.4 How to ask for a refund

Write to support@couragelibrary.in, or raise a ticket from Help, with your registered email, the payment date and the Razorpay payment ID (on your receipt). We acknowledge within 48 hours and decide within 7 working days.

### 3.5 How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We do not refund in cash or to a different account.
