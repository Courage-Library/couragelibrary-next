# Platform Overview & Product Vision
**Comprehensive Product Overview, Target Examination Domains & Core Value Proposition**

---

## 1. Product Vision & Mission

**Courage Library** is built to democratize high-quality, authentic, self-paced competitive examination preparation across India.

Millions of aspirants prepare annually for central and state government recruitment exams (SSC, UPSC, Railways, Banking, Defence, State PSCs). These candidates frequently encounter:
1. Fragmented, outdated, and contradictory exam information across unofficial coaching blogs.
2. Rote learning without personalized cognitive error tracking.
3. Disconnected mock tests that fail to diagnose *why* mistakes occur.

Courage Library solves these problems through an integrated, authoritative ecosystem combining:
- **Verified Examination Knowledge**: Exhaustive, official-source-verified intelligence for 24 knowledge modules per exam.
- **Canonical Curriculum Lessons**: Structured learning units covering theory, solved examples, and formulas.
- **Computerized Adaptive & Live Testing**: High-concurrency mock engine with authentic scoring and national ranking.
- **Mistake Vault**: Cognitive error taxonomy and spaced-repetition relapse drills turning errors into permanent mastery.

## 3. Key User Personas & Roles

### 3.1 Serious Aspirant (Candidate / Student)
- **Goals**: Understand exam eligibility and pattern, study high-yield topics, benchmark performance against national cohorts, identify cognitive weak spots, eliminate unforced errors.
- **Key Features**: Exam Hub, Interactive Course Reader, Daily & Full-Length Mocks, Mistake Vault, Relapse Drills, Gamification Coins & Badges.

### 3.2 Academic Reviewer & Subject Matter Expert (Staff)
- **Goals**: Curate curriculum, review AI-drafted knowledge documents, verify official notification citations, validate question psychometrics, resolve reported errata.
- **Key Features**: Review Workbench, 5-Gate Validation Previews, Semantic Diff Engine, Question Errata Manager.

### 3.3 Platform Administrator (Admin)
- **Goals**: Onboard new examinations, manage recruitment cycles, monitor 14-dimension readiness gates, oversee subscription tiers and platform health.
- **Key Features**: Unified Multi-Exam Onboarding Studio, Readiness Gate Panel, User Role Manager, Platform Analytics.
